Features

Solar Bowl Kitchen

A Safer, cleaner Path to Global Food Security — an environmentally friendly, emission-free, and sustainable method of preparing food that acts as a direct substitute for fossil fuels and biomass, lowering greenhouse gas emissions. Unlike other options that lose heat into surrounding air, this keeps the cooking area cool and less humid.

Steam Cooked Food

Cooking with steam helps minimize nutrient loss and the amount of harmful compounds which usually happens while cooking at extremely high temperature.
